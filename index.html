Meet the Artist

Hyper-realism is about more than just copying a photo; it's about capturing the essence and the soul of the subject. I focus on the micro-details that make a piece truly come alive—the way light hits a pupil, the texture of skin, or the subtle shadows in a portrait.

My goal is to create art that defies the medium of skin. I want people to look at your tattoo and question if it's a photograph.

It's a role I grew into and like my markings, earnt over decades. Although its roots are ancient I share my gift through its modern personification. - Albatross
